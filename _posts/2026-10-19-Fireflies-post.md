**What exactly do I mean by "Fireflies"?**

I was assigned with taking three separate assignments and integrating them into one piece of code. Each assignment was to code a program that lit an led 
on an arduino in a pattern that matched different fireflies in real life. The first assignment was for fireflies that fade in their brightness uniformly over 3
seconds, then go off for three seconds. The second assignment was for fireflies that turn on for half a second every 4 seconds, and are otherwise off. Finally,
the last assignment was for a firefly that is always on, but whose brightness randomly either increases or decreases every half second. What would've helped me 
with this assignment was checking that none of my variable names across previous files were the same (they were). So, when I pasted different bits of code in, 
they interacted with each other unintentionally. Once I realized, I changed my brightness variable to brightness1, brightness2, and brightness3.
