## The Butterfly

This butterfly is a simple circuit that turns on 2 LEDs when a button is pressed. This is a rendition of the butterfly I saw a frog staring at on the banks of
the Kokosing. This butterfly is my example of complex embroidery with long, intricate, running stitch patterns and a satin stitch in the middle of the butterfly. 
There are two main things I would do differently for next time: First, I would trim my ends of conductive fabric as I make them, and I would use shorter pieces of 
conductive fabric. I spent so much time dealing with conductive fabric that dragged an end in the wrong place or shorted my circuit that it would definitely
have been worthwhile to just work with more manageable pieces.
