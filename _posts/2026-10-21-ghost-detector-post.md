**What exacty is a Ghost Detector?**

So, as a spookier time of year approaches (Halloween is just a week away), there exists a natural urge to understand the supernatural around us. On a completely unrelated note, I have been assigned a task: make red arduino leds turn on when a temperature and light sensor read abnormally low values. It 
turns out this is only a little confusing. Unlike the light sensor, the temperature sensor sends out readings that aren't scaled directly to 
temperature, but are shifted. So, I had to learn how to convert the temperature sensor readings to actual temperatures. For the future, I would use the serial monitor for temperature as well as the brightness. I thought they would behave similarly so that after reading brightness values, I didn't have to check temperatures, but that was not the case in the slightest.
