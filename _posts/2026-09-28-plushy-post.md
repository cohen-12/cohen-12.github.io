**What am I on about this time?**

For my next trick, I'm going to pull a rabbit out of my hat. By trick I mean assignment, and by a rabbit out of a hat, I mean a big hero 6 looking plushy from
hours of sewing and some raw materials. To be clear, one (at least of my skill level) doesn't just sit down with some felt, thread, a couple needles (and needle
threaders) and a few hours later, a plushy appears. Rather, if you look at exhibits A and B, you can see me sketching out my circuit on paper, and then actually
testing them with wires before I sew the circuit together. Point being, I planned this out. The task at hand was to make a light up plushy with a button, lights,
switch, and a battery, while adding decorative elements to the design. I planned out not only my circuit, but also my decorations, and where they could be on the
plushy so that I'd not interfere with my closing up the plushy in the future. Then, I executed my plan, though not without hiccups. One light of mine
refused to work consistently, and although I initially overlooked this possibility due to my other two lights looking fine, it turns out my battery was poorly
connected to the rest of the circuit. Once I fixed this, executing the rest of my plan went smoothly. One thing I would do differently next time is cut shorter
segments of conductive thread, as I started by cutting long ones and then spending 10 minutes untangling them, and it would have been faster to just cut short pieces.
