**My Project**

I was tasked with coming up with three pieces that together form a thematically united project. The pieces are each worked examples of what I've learned from 
electronic textiles. I was inspired by a walk along the kokosing, where I saw a frog on the riverbank entranced by a butterfly. When a classmate of mine brought
up that frogs are their favorite animal, I was instantly reminded of this moment, and decided to make it into my project. I plan to make a frog that ribbits with
the flip of a switch, and whose eyes twinkle when placed on the lilypad. The lilypad is actually a homemade button connected to an arduino, and the butterfly
is going to be a specially decorated piece that lights up when a button is pressed.
