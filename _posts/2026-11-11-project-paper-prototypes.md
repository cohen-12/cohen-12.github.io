**A Riverbank Adventure**
This collection of pieces represents a vivid scene from a walk by the kokosing. In this scene, a frog on a lilypad is captivated by a butterfly's bright colors.

The three pieces are "The Frog", "The Lilypad", and "The Butterfly". 

The frog functions as follows. On top of the frog, there is a switch that makes the frog ribbit when flipped. Further, when the frog is connected to the lilypad
by the snaps, the light sensor functions as an input, controlling the LEDs representing the frog's eyes. 

Image:

The lilypad is a homemade button wired to the arduino. It also has two snaps and a light sensor, also wired to the arduino. When the button is pressed, and the
frog snaps are pressed into the lilypad snaps, the arduino reads in from the light sensor and sends out data to the snaps, which control the frog LEDs.

Image

The butterfly is an ornately embroidered piece of fabric with a battery, button, and two LEDs that light up when the button is pressed.
