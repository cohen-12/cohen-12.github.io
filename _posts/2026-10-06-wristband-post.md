**My Wristband**

My project this time was a wristband made primarily of felt. The wristband had to light up when it was closed and a homemade button (A button made out of common components) was pressed. For my homemade button, I put one piece of conductive fabric a height above another piece of conductive fabric, with a non conductive spacer in between. When pressed, the conductive fabric on top can touch the bottom fabric, closing the circuit. My project was made to look like a flower, where the center of the flower lights up when everything is connected. However, making lights light up in fabric is difficult when they are very close together, so as I prototyped, I noticed this and adjusted my design to provide more space between the lights, which allowed them to work. For the future, I would make sure to be accurate in cutting out the right size of wristband, because when I first did it, it ended up being too small. It's easier to trim something too long down to size than to add back fabric that was cut off.
